Why African History Belongs in Children's Education

September 25, 2026

🌍

Every child deserves the opportunity to understand the story of the world they live in. For African children, that story includes the long and diverse history of the African continent.

Learning African history can introduce children to civilizations, communities, leaders, thinkers, artists, inventors and ordinary people whose experiences helped shape the continent.

Africa Has a Rich History

Africa's history stretches across thousands of years and includes many different societies, cultures and traditions. It cannot be reduced to a single story.

Teaching children about this diversity can help them appreciate the many communities and civilizations that have existed across the continent.

History Helps Children Understand Identity

Knowing where people come from can help children understand their communities, traditions and heritage.

History can give young people opportunities to ask important questions about identity, culture and how societies change over time.

Learning From the Past

History is not only about remembering dates and names. It can help children examine decisions, achievements, challenges and changes from the past.

When taught thoughtfully, history can encourage children to think critically about the world around them.

Building a Connection With Heritage

African history can also create opportunities for children to explore stories from their own communities and discover the people and traditions that came before them.

This makes history more personal, meaningful and connected to everyday life.

The Success Land Vision

At Success Land, we believe children should have the opportunity to learn about Africa as part of understanding the wider world. Our goal is to encourage curiosity, knowledge and appreciation for the history and heritage of the continent.
